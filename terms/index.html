Press Release For Business is a paid publishing wire. You pay us to publish your announcement at a permanent address on this website, structured with machine-readable data and distributed as described on the distribution page.

3. Submission and editorial discretion

Submitting an announcement does not oblige us to publish it. We review everything against our editorial guidelines and may decline it, or ask you to change it, for any reason consistent with those guidelines. Where we decline after payment, you are refunded in full.

We may decline without giving a detailed reason where doing so would require us to make claims about your business we cannot substantiate.

4. Payment, revisions and refunds

  • Nothing is charged at submission. Payment is taken after you have seen and approved the final document.
  • One editorial pass is included at the Optimized and Network tiers. That pass is the service you are buying: we restructure your material and send it back with notes before anything publishes.
  • Corrections of our errors are free, always. If we introduced a mistake, we fix it at no charge and there is no time limit on that.
  • Changes you request after you have approved the document cost $25 each. This includes rewrites of material you supplied, and edits to a release that is already live.
  • Refunds. Full refund if we decline your announcement after payment. Once a release is published at your approval, the work is delivered and the fee is not refundable. If you want it taken down, see section 8.
