Why this exists

Wire distribution died. The format didn’t.

Blasting an announcement to four hundred sites stopped working over a decade ago. What survived is the structure underneath — and it turns out to suit machine retrieval almost exactly.

Dated and attributed

An explicit publication date, a named issuing organization and a named spokesperson. Retrieval systems will not cite a claim they cannot attribute or place in time.

Written to be extracted

The opening passage is constructed to be accurate and complete on its own, because that is frequently all a system takes before it moves on.

Structurally explicit

Markup that states the document type, the publisher, the entities involved and the dates — rather than leaving a machine to infer any of it.
