Privacy policy

The short version: no accounts, no reader database, and the forms on this site open your own email client rather than sending anything to a server.

What we collect

Nothing, in the ordinary course of reading. This is a static site. There is no login, no account and no server-side record of who visited what.

Forms on this site assemble your entries into a message and open your own email application. Nothing reaches us until you press send in your own client, and what arrives is an ordinary email in an ordinary mailbox.

If you submit a release

We keep the correspondence and the published document. The contact details you ask us to publish in a release — typically a media contact name, phone and email — are published because you asked us to publish them. Everything else stays in correspondence.

Payment

When payment processing is active, it is handled by a third-party processor. Card details are entered on the processor's systems and are never transmitted to or stored by us. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the associated order reference.

Third-party requests

Some resources load from other organizations. When that happens your browser contacts them directly and they can see the request.

We set no advertising cookies of our own and run no third-party analytics or tracking pixels on this site.

Advertising

Advertising on this site promotes Eye To Ad Media, which operates this publication, and is served from our own pages with no third-party ad technology. Placements are labelled.

Your rights

Because we hold no reader database, most data requests have a simple answer: we have nothing about you. If you have corresponded with us, ask and we will delete it — except where a release has been published, which is a public document.
