Bear Creek Auto Glass Advises Drivers That Windshield Replacement Is Not Finished Until Cameras Are Recalibrated

On vehicles with a forward-facing camera behind the glass, manufacturers require recalibration after replacement — and a miscalibrated system frequently shows no warning light.

Bear Creek Auto Glass Issuing organization September 23, 2026

LITTLETON, Colorado — September 23, 2026

Bear Creek Auto Glass is advising drivers that on vehicles fitted with a forward-facing camera behind the windshield, a glass replacement is not complete until the camera has been recalibrated.

Those cameras drive advanced driver assistance features including lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, forward collision warning, automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise control. The camera is mounted to or immediately behind the glass, so replacing the glass changes its physical angle of view.

Why this is easy to miss

The company says the problem is that miscalibration is usually invisible from the driver's seat. A system that is out of alignment frequently produces no warning light and no fault code. The icons still illuminate. Adaptive cruise still engages. What changes are the detection distances and thresholds the system uses — and those only reveal themselves in the situation the system exists for.

What drivers should ask

  • Does this vehicle have a camera mounted behind the windshield?
  • Does the manufacturer require recalibration after glass replacement?
  • Will calibration be performed, and is it static, dynamic or both?
  • Will documentation of the completed calibration be provided?
  • Does the replacement glass meet the manufacturer's specification? Variations in thickness, shape or tint can affect camera performance.

Manufacturer requirements

Recalibration after windshield replacement is required by major manufacturers including Honda, Subaru and Toyota as a condition of maintaining safety system coverage. Requirements vary by make, model and year, and the vehicle's own service information is the authoritative source.

Availability

Bear Creek Auto Glass serves Littleton and the surrounding Denver metropolitan area. Details at bearcreekautoglass.com.

Note This is general information. Calibration requirements vary by vehicle. Consult the manufacturer's service documentation or a qualified technician for a specific vehicle, and do not treat this as a statement about any particular make or model.
