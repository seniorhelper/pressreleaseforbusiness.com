My AI Agent Army Opens an AI Agent Factory and Publishes the Real Output of 17 Built-and-Tested Agents

My AI Agent Army opens an AI agent factory for businesses and publishes real reports and drafts from 17 agents it has already built and tested.

My AI Agent Army Issuing organization October 15, 2026

DENVER, Colo., October 15, 2026 — My AI Agent Army, an AI agent factory operated by Denver's Eye To Ad Media, is now taking orders at myaiagentarmy.com. Businesses can order a single agent or hundreds, either ready-made from the company's agent templates or custom-built from scratch. With the launch, the company published the actual reports and drafts from 17 agents it has already built and tested, run on a sample plumbing company, at myaiagentarmy.com/see-them-work.

Most AI agent offers today are demos or chat windows. The sample-output page is meant to show what the agents produce before anyone buys: a morning briefing that caught a double booking, replies drafted to three overnight leads, a three-month profit and loss built from raw bank exports, a renewal report that flagged an expired insurance policy, and an AI-readiness audit with the fix files it wrote. The business and its customers are made up; the output is not edited.

The 17 agents already built

  • Operations: Daily Briefing, Meeting to Actions (transcripts into tasks with owners and due dates), Desktop Organizer.
  • Sales and service: Speed-to-Lead Responder, Review Request, Customer Reactivation.
  • Admin and finance: Invoice Reminder with an aging report, P&L Builder, Spend Leak Finder (price increases, duplicate charges, overlapping tools), Renewal Sentinel (domains, SSL certificates, licenses, insurance and contract cancel-by dates).
  • Marketing: Google Business Profile poster, Content Repurposer, AI Visibility (whether AI assistants recommend a business), Listing Consistency.
  • Web and AI readiness: Site Health, and the Agent-Ready Auditor, which scores how ready a business is for AI assistants and agents that search, compare and book on a customer's behalf, then writes the fixes.
  • Command: Commander, which watches the other agents and sends one daily digest.

Every agent drafts first. Emails, texts and posts wait in an approval queue until a person approves them, every action is logged, and each agent keeps working with or without an AI model connected. The suite runs more than 300 automated tests covering its math, edge cases and safety rules.

We didn't want to sell an agent that only works in a demo. So we built them, tested them on a sample business and put the real output on the site. If you can read what an agent did before you buy it, you can decide for yourself whether it's worth handing that job off.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Eye To Ad Media.

Pricing and ownership

Ready-made agents start at $1,500 to own for a Scout, $3,500 for a Specialist and $7,500 for a Commander, or on Subscribe-to-Own from $149, $299 or $599 a month per agent plus a one-time setup of $500, $1,000 or $1,500. Custom-built agents start at $4,000 for a single workflow, $7,500 to $15,000 for multi-step agents and $15,000 for custom multi-agent teams. Every project starts with a $750 Agent Blueprint, credited toward the build. Deployment is scoped and priced in writing after the Blueprint. Clients own their agent's setup and all of their data, and custom code written for a client is theirs.

Prices and the full roster of 43 agent templates are at myaiagentarmy.com, or call 1-800-481-8638.
