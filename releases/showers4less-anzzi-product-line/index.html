Showers4Less Adds ANZZI Shower Products to Its Accessible Bathing Catalog

The supplier is now an authorized dealer for the line, widening the pan, enclosure and fixture options available alongside its existing barrier-free range.

Showers4Less Issuing organization September 20, 2026

At a glance

Showers4Less has become an authorized ANZZI dealer and added the line's shower products to its catalog, widening the pan, enclosure and fixture options available alongside its existing barrier-free range.

DENVER, Colorado — September 20, 2026

Showers4Less has added ANZZI shower products to its catalog. The company is an authorized dealer for the line, which sits alongside its existing barrier-free and accessible shower range.

The addition widens the pan, enclosure and fixture options available, particularly for households whose bathroom dimensions ruled out the configurations previously carried.

"A lot of accessible bathroom projects stall on fit," said Zachary Tye Wennstedt of Showers4Less. "The right product exists and it just isn't the one you stock. More of the catalog means fewer of those conversations end badly."

What to confirm before ordering

The company advises confirming the following on any shower system, whichever manufacturer it comes from:

  • Threshold height against the actual access requirement — ambulatory, walker or wheelchair
  • Pan and wall system are a matched set from the same manufacturer
  • Reinforcement present at intended grab bar positions
  • Drain location against what the existing plumbing allows
  • Slip resistance rating of the pan surface when wet

Availability

Product information and quotes are available at Showers4Less.com. Walk-in tubs and full accessible bathing configurations are available through Aging Safely Baths.

Frequently asked

What is an authorized dealer?

An authorized dealer has a direct supply relationship with the manufacturer, including permission to sell the products and to use the manufacturer's product content and imagery. It is distinct from reselling stock acquired through a third party.

Can shower pans and enclosures from different manufacturers be combined?

It is generally not advisable. Pans and wall systems are engineered as matched sets, and mixing across manufacturers frequently produces sealing problems at the pan-to-wall joint — the point most likely to fail and the most expensive place to discover a failure.

Does Showers4Less ship nationwide?

Yes. The company supplies barrier-free and accessible shower systems to households and contractors across the United States.
