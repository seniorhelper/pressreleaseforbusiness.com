Painting Brighton Publishes Guidance on When Cabinet Refinishing Works and When It Does Not

Refinishing costs a fraction of replacement, and there are specific conditions under which it is the wrong choice anyway.

Painting Brighton Issuing organization August 30, 2026

At a glance

Painting Brighton has published guidance on cabinet refinishing, setting out which cabinet conditions refinishing suits and the specific cases where replacement is the better decision despite the higher cost.

BRIGHTON, Colorado — August 30, 2026

Painting Brighton, operating under Painter Hotline LLC, has published guidance on kitchen cabinet refinishing, covering both when it is the right decision and when it is not.

The company says refinishing is frequently the better value and is occasionally sold to people whose cabinets should be replaced instead.

When refinishing is the right call

  • The boxes are structurally sound and square
  • Doors and drawer fronts are solid wood or a paintable veneer
  • The layout works and is not being changed
  • The finish is dated or worn rather than damaged
  • Hinges and slides work or can be replaced independently

When it is not

  • Water damage to the boxes. Swollen or delaminating particleboard does not recover, and coating it hides a problem rather than fixing it.
  • Thermofoil or laminate surfaces. These do not take coatings reliably without specialist preparation, and a poor result here is very visible.
  • Failing joinery. If boxes are racking or joints are opening, the finish is not the problem.
  • A layout that does not work. Refinishing a kitchen somebody dislikes the shape of produces a nicer-looking kitchen they still dislike.

On cure time

The company notes that the most common cause of a cabinet finish failing within months is insufficient cure time between stages. Cabinets are handled daily, frequently with wet or greasy hands, and a coating that has not fully hardened will not survive that.

"Nobody wants to hear that their kitchen is out of action for longer," the company said. "But a finish that chips in February because we rushed it in January helps nobody."

Availability

Painting Brighton serves Brighton and the surrounding Adams County area. Estimates are available at paintingbrighton.com.

Frequently asked

Is cabinet refinishing cheaper than replacement?

Substantially, in most cases, because the cabinet boxes stay in place and no demolition, disposal or new installation is involved. The saving only holds where the boxes are sound — refinishing a failing box is money spent on something that still has to be replaced later.

Which cabinets cannot be refinished well?

Thermofoil and laminate surfaces do not take coatings reliably without specialist preparation, and boxes with water damage, delaminating particleboard or failing joinery are poor candidates regardless of the door material.

How long does cabinet refinishing take?

Longer than most homeowners expect, because coatings on cabinets need proper cure time between stages and the surfaces get handled daily afterward. Rushing the cure is the most common cause of a finish that chips within months.
