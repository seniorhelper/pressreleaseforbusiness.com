Eye To Ad Media Expands Generative Engine Optimization Practice as AI Assistants Reshape How Customers Find Local Businesses

The Denver agency, founded in 2012, is formalizing a service line built around entity consistency, structured publishing and machine-readable content — the mechanics that determine whether a business appears inside an AI-generated answer.

Eye To Ad Media Issuing organization September 25, 2026

At a glance

Eye To Ad Media, a Denver marketing agency founded in 2012, has expanded its generative engine optimization practice into a formal service line covering entity consistency, structured publishing, answer-shaped content and machine-readable site infrastructure for businesses across the United States.

DENVER, Colorado — September 25, 2026

Eye To Ad Media, a Denver marketing agency founded in 2012, has expanded its generative engine optimization practice into a formal service line for businesses whose customers have started asking AI assistants the questions they used to type into a search box.

The expansion reflects a shift the agency says it has been tracking across client accounts for roughly two years: a growing share of commercial research now happens inside AI assistants and AI-generated search summaries, where a business is either present in the answer or absent from it entirely. There is no second page.

"Ranking was always a position on a list," said Zachary Tye Wennstedt, founder and chief executive officer of Eye To Ad Media. "An answer isn't a list. Either the system has enough verifiable information about your business to include you, or it doesn't and it names somebody else. That's a different problem, and it needs different work."

What the practice covers

The service line is built around four areas of work, all of which concern how a business is represented to systems that read rather than browse.

Entity consistency

Retrieval systems build confidence about an organization by corroborating the same facts across independent sources. Where a business name, address, service description or ownership detail differs between its own site, directory listings, social profiles and third-party mentions, that inconsistency reduces confidence. The agency's entity work involves auditing every discoverable representation of a business and reconciling them to a single accurate version.

Structured publishing

Explicit structured data tells a machine what a page is about rather than requiring it to infer. The practice covers organization and service markup, publication and modification dates, identity linking between a business's verifiable presences, and the article-level markup that determines whether a piece of content can be attributed and cited.

Answer-shaped content

Content written to be extracted has different properties from content written to be scrolled. The agency's editorial work emphasizes opening passages that are accurate and complete in isolation, claims that are specific enough to be verified, and the replacement of promotional adjectives with facts that can be checked.

Machine-readable site infrastructure

This includes crawler directives for AI systems, plain-language site summaries at the locations assistants look for them, canonical URL discipline, and the technical work that determines whether content is reachable and parseable at all.

Most small businesses have exactly one source of information about themselves that a machine can read, and it's their own website. That's a thin evidence base. The work is making that base wider and making every part of it agree. Zachary Tye Wennstedt, Founder & CEO, Eye To Ad Media

Why smaller businesses are affected differently

Large organizations are already well represented in the sources AI systems draw on. They are covered by trade press, they file public documents, and they are written about by parties other than themselves. A retrieval system assembling an answer about a national brand has abundant corroborating material.

Smaller businesses frequently have none of that. What exists about them is their own website, a handful of directory entries and whatever reviews have accumulated. Where those sources disagree — an old address on one listing, a service the business no longer offers on another — the disagreement itself becomes a reason to omit the business from an answer.

Eye To Ad Media says this asymmetry is the central practical argument for the work: the businesses with the least existing coverage have the most to gain from making the coverage they do have consistent and machine-readable.

Scope of the practice

  • Entity audit across owned properties, directories and third-party mentions
  • Reconciliation of name, address, contact and service data to a single accurate version
  • Organization, service and article structured data implementation
  • Identity linking between verifiable business presences
  • Editorial revision of core pages toward extractable, verifiable claims
  • Crawler directive configuration for AI and search systems
  • Plain-language site summary files at the conventional locations
  • Ongoing monitoring of how the business is represented in assistant responses

Operator background

Eye To Ad Media's client concentration in home services and the trades reflects its founder's professional history. Before establishing the agency, Wennstedt worked as a sales manager for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies and managed national remodeling companies and their sales teams.

The firm also operates its own businesses, including Aging Safely Baths and Showers4Less, and states that it tests marketing approaches on those properties before deploying them for clients.

"We've been on the other side of the invoice," Wennstedt said. "I've hired agencies that charged a lot and delivered a report nobody read. When you've paid for that yourself, you build the service differently."

Frequently asked

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization is the practice of making a business's information consistent, verifiable and machine-readable so that AI assistants and AI-generated search summaries can identify and cite it accurately. It differs from conventional search optimization in that the objective is inclusion in an answer rather than position on a list of results.

Why does entity consistency matter for AI search?

Retrieval systems build confidence about an organization by corroborating the same facts across independent sources. Where a business name, address, service description or ownership detail differs between its own site, directory listings and third-party mentions, that inconsistency reduces confidence and can result in the business being omitted from an answer entirely.

Do smaller businesses benefit more or less from this work?

Smaller businesses typically have a thinner evidence base — often just their own website and a handful of directory entries — which means inconsistencies carry proportionally more weight. Large organizations already have abundant third-party corroboration from trade press, public filings and independent coverage.
