World VR Mall Opens Merchant Storefront Applications

Retailers can apply for a space on the browser-based concourse, with checkout remaining entirely on the merchant's own site.

Eye To Ad Media Issuing organization September 5, 2026

DENVER, Colorado — September 5, 2026

World VR Mall has opened storefront applications to retailers. The mall is a browser-based three-dimensional shopping concourse requiring no headset or download from visitors.

Merchants keep their existing ecommerce operation entirely. The mall handles discovery; visitors who decide to buy are handed to the merchant's own checkout.

What participation involves

  • No catalog integration or order synchronization
  • No transaction fee and no marketplace terms
  • No change to payment processing, fulfilllment or returns
  • The merchant retains the customer relationship

"We're not trying to be a marketplace," said Zachary Tye Wennstedt, founder of World VR Mall. "Marketplaces take a cut forever and have to justify it forever. We drive people to your own shop. That's a simpler thing to defend."

Availability

Applications are open at worldvrmall.com.
