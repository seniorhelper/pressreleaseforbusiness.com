Roof Solutions Urges Metro Atlanta Homeowners to Photograph Roofs Before Storm Season as Georgia Insurance Rules Shift in 2026

New Georgia legislation, a City of Atlanta cool roof ordinance and tightening carrier treatment of older roofs make documented roof condition more consequential than it has been in years.

Roof Solutions Issuing organization September 25, 2026

At a glance

Roof Solutions is advising Metro Atlanta homeowners to establish dated photographic documentation of roof condition ahead of storm season, citing 2026 changes to Georgia insurance legislation, a City of Atlanta cool roof ordinance and increased carrier scrutiny of roofs over 15 years old.

ATLANTA, Georgia — September 25, 2026

Roof Solutions, an industrial, commercial and residential roofing contractor serving Metro Atlanta, is advising homeowners to establish photographic documentation of their roof's current condition, citing several 2026 changes to how Georgia roof insurance claims are assessed and paid.

The company's argument is narrow and practical: an adjuster deciding whether damage is new or pre-existing is making a judgment call, and a homeowner with dated photographs of the roof before the storm is in a materially different position from one without.

What changed in 2026

Several developments have shifted the ground under Georgia roof claims this year.

Catastrophe Savings Accounts

Georgia House Bill 511, effective January 1, 2026, permits residents to use Catastrophe Savings Accounts to pay deductibles and storm-recovery costs with tax-advantaged funds. Roof Solutions advises homeowners to consult a tax professional about how the provision applies to their circumstances.

Wind mitigation discounts

Georgia House Bill 279 requires insurers to offer discounts for wind mitigation measures, reported in the range of 10% to 28%. Impact-resistant Class 4 shingles are among the qualifying measures, which can change the economics of a replacement decision for homeowners already facing one.

Atlanta cool roof ordinance

A City of Atlanta cool roof ordinance taking effect in 2026 means code-compliant replacements within the city may require specific materials. Homeowners whose policies include ordinance or law coverage — which pays the additional cost of bringing a repaired or replaced roof up to current code — may find that coverage newly relevant.

Carrier treatment of older roofs

Industry reporting through 2026 indicates Georgia homeowners with roofs 15 years or older are facing increased scrutiny during claims, with some carriers offering actual cash value rather than replacement cost payouts on aging roofs. Storm damage remains a covered peril regardless of roof age, but the question of whether observed damage is storm-caused or age-related becomes more consequential as a roof gets older.

An adjuster looking at a fifteen-year-old roof has to decide whether what they are seeing happened last Tuesday or over the last decade. If you have photographs from before the storm, that stops being a debate. Roof Solutions

The documentation argument

Roof Solutions structures its own estimates around photographs, providing homeowners with images of the actual roof — soft spots, lifted shingles, failing flashing detail — before discussing scope or cost.

The company says the practice originated as a sales decision and turned out to serve a second purpose: homeowners who have an inspection on file have a dated, third-party record of roof condition, which is precisely what is useful if a storm arrives afterward.

What Metro Atlanta homeowners can do now

  • Review the declarations page — know the wind and hail deductible percentage and whether a cosmetic damage exclusion applies
  • Establish a dated photographic record of current roof condition
  • Check whether the policy includes ordinance or law coverage, newly relevant inside Atlanta
  • Confirm whether the policy pays actual cash value or replacement cost, particularly on roofs over 15 years old
  • Report damage within days of a storm rather than months — delay makes attribution to a specific event harder
  • Ask about wind mitigation discounts before committing to materials on a replacement

Storm frequency

Hail is a recurring rather than exceptional event across Metro Atlanta, with damage clustering along the region's storm corridors. Roof Solutions notes that the practical consequence for homeowners is that the question is generally not whether a roof will be tested but when — which is an argument for documenting condition during calm weather rather than after.
