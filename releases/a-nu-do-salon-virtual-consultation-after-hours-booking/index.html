A Nu Do Salon Expands Virtual Consultations as Industry Data Shows Roughly Half of Salon Bookings Happen After Hours

Published 2026 benchmarks put after-hours booking demand at 46% to 50% of all appointments. The Brighton salon is pairing round-the-clock booking with pre-appointment virtual consultations for color work.

A Nu Do Salon Issuing organization September 24, 2026

BRIGHTON, Colorado — September 24, 2026

A Nu Do Salon, a full-service hair salon in Brighton, Colorado, has expanded its virtual consultation service, allowing clients to discuss color goals, hair history and maintenance expectations with a stylist before their appointment rather than during it.

The expansion responds to two documented shifts in how salon clients behave. Published 2026 industry benchmarks put after-hours booking demand at roughly 46% to 50% of all appointments — meaning close to half of bookings are made when salons are physically closed. Separately, industry analysis through 2026 has identified in-depth consultation as among the defining changes in salon service, replacing the rushed pre-service conversation with a structured discussion of undertone, chemical history and maintenance tolerance.

Why consultation moved to the front

The salon says the change is most consequential for color work, where the gap between what a client pictures and what is achievable on their hair is the most common source of disappointment.

Industry guidance through 2026 has emphasized that setting maintenance expectations at the start — showing a client what their color will look like at six, ten and fourteen weeks rather than only on the day — reduces both dissatisfaction and the corrective work that follows it.

Most color problems are not technical. They are a conversation that did not happen, or happened in five minutes with foils already mixed. A Nu Do Salon

What the salon offers

A Nu Do Salon is a full-service salon carrying Eufora professional products. Services and features include:

  • Cutting, color and styling for a range of hair types and textures
  • Virtual consultations ahead of appointments, particularly for color and correction work
  • Online booking available outside business hours
  • UV tanning services
  • Eufora professional product retail
  • Gift cards

What a pre-appointment consultation covers

  • Chemical history — what has previously been on the hair and when
  • Current condition and what it will tolerate
  • Target tone and whether it is reachable in one session
  • Maintenance tolerance — how often the client will realistically return
  • What the result looks like at six, ten and fourteen weeks
  • Time and cost, before anyone is in the chair

The after-hours problem

The salon notes that the booking figure is the part most independent salons underestimate. A salon that takes bookings only by telephone during opening hours is unreachable for roughly half the moments when clients decide to book — and a client who cannot book at nine in the evening frequently books somewhere else instead.

Published benchmarks suggest salons should target 60% to 70% of visits booked online to meet that demand, and industry commentary through 2026 has framed digital conversion rather than front desk capacity as the practical growth constraint for independent salons.

Availability

A Nu Do Salon is located in Brighton, Colorado. Appointments, virtual consultations and gift cards are available at anudosalon.com.
