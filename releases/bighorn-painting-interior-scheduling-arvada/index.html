Bighorn Painting Opens Interior Booking for the Winter Season in Jefferson County

Interior work is unaffected by the temperature constraints that close the Colorado exterior window, and scheduling improves once exterior projects stop.

Bighorn Painting Issuing organization September 12, 2026

ARVADA, Colorado — September 12, 2026

Bighorn Painting has opened interior project booking for the winter season across Arvada and Jefferson County.

Interior work is unaffected by the temperature constraints that close the Colorado exterior season each October, and the company notes that scheduling is generally easier once exterior projects stop competing for crew time.

What winter is well suited to

  • Whole-home interior repaints
  • Cabinet and trim work, which benefits from controlled conditions
  • Ceiling repair and settling cracks accumulated over the year
  • Basement and garage interiors

Preparation

The company advises homeowners to identify any moisture staining or cracking before a quote rather than after, since those need addressing beneath the coating rather than over it. Rooms should be cleared or furniture centralized, and wall fixtures removed where practical.

On exterior work

Homeowners planning exterior projects for next year are advised to book early. In HOA-governed neighborhoods, color approval can take weeks, and beginning the process in spring frequently means joining a queue.

Availability

Estimates can be scheduled at bighornpainting.com or 720-912-4676.
