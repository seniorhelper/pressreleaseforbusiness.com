Search Converts Details Membership Structure for Small Business Growth Program Built Around Strategy Sessions and Community Distribution

The program is structured as a membership rather than a media buy, with strategy review, campaign testing and distribution across community networks among the stated benefits.

Search Converts Issuing organization September 23, 2026

DENVER, Colorado — September 23, 2026

Search Converts has published details of the structure behind its business growth accelerator, a membership program for small businesses that combines marketing strategy review with distribution through community networks. The program is structured as a membership rather than as an advertising buy.

The distinction is deliberate. Members join a program that includes access and advisory work; they are not purchasing advertising placement as a commodity.

What membership includes

Strategy session

Members receive a thirty-minute working session with a marketing specialist covering call-to-action construction, design variation, structured testing and distribution planning. The company describes the session as diagnostic rather than presentational — the objective is to identify what is failing in a member's current approach rather than to deliver a recommendation deck.

Campaign testing

The program includes structured testing of creative and messaging variations, an approach that larger advertisers treat as routine and that smaller businesses typically skip because the setup overhead exceeds what a single campaign justifies.

Community distribution

Members receive visibility within community buy-sell-trade networks that the company administers, including groups in the Omaha metropolitan area. The company is direct about why this is structured as a membership benefit rather than sold as placement.

"Those groups work because members trust other members," said Zachary Tye Wennstedt. "The moment you turn them into an advertising channel and sell slots, the people who made them valuable leave, and then you've got nothing worth selling. I'm not doing that to groups I've spent years building."

You cannot buy your way into a community. You can be a member of one. The program is built on that distinction and it isn't negotiable. Zachary Tye Wennstedt, Search Converts

Format guidance

Members receive guidance on the group cover photo format, in which posts default to a group's cover image. The company reports that because many posters leave the default in place, member content in that format is frequently reshared by other participants — a distribution effect that depends on the content reading as member content rather than as an advertisement.

Services delivered

Included in the service range

  • Marketing strategy sessions covering CTAs, design variation and testing
  • Structured A/B testing of creative and messaging
  • Community network visibility as a membership benefit
  • Self-publishing options for members
  • Posting on behalf of members where requested
  • Business page administration
  • Advertising pixel configuration
  • Paid media: pay-per-click, display and remarketing campaigns

Positioning

Search Converts states that the program is aimed at owner-operated businesses that lack the volume to justify a conventional agency retainer but need more structure than self-directed marketing typically produces.

The company notes that its approach reflects direct experience on the client side. "I've hired agencies that took a lot of money and produced a report," Wennstedt said. "I've also made my own mistakes with my own money. Both of those are more useful to a member than a case study would be."

The company also states that it tests marketing approaches on its own businesses before deploying them for members, and that members sometimes receive additional advertising at no charge where the company is evaluating an approach.

Availability

Program details and membership enquiries are available at searchconverts.com or by telephone at 1-800-481-8638.
