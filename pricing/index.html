Nine things we do that Standard doesn’t

Every item below is work done to the document, not decoration added to the page. Standard releases are clean, properly typeset and fully labeled — we do not make them worse to sell you an upgrade.

What Optimized buys is structure a machine can use.

01 · Editorial

The opening passage is rebuilt to stand alone

We rewrite the first forty words so they are accurate and complete with nothing else around them — who, what, where, when.

Why: a retrieval system frequently extracts only the opening passage. If yours opens with positioning, positioning is what gets quoted.

02 · Editorial

Unverifiable claims become checkable facts

“Leading regional provider” becomes a specific, sourced statement — or it comes out. Every adjective is either converted or removed.

Why: systems cannot cite what they cannot verify. Unverifiable claims are not just wasted words, they displace usable ones.

03 · Structure

An “at a glance” summary block

A single self-contained paragraph summarizing the announcement, placed above the body and marked up as the document’s abstract.

Why: gives extraction a clean, deliberate target instead of letting it guess which paragraph matters.

04 · Structure

A frequently-asked section, marked as FAQ

Three or more question-and-answer pairs drawn from your announcement, published as an expandable section and declared as FAQPage data.

Why: people ask assistants questions. Question-and-answer pairs map directly onto that, and FAQ markup is separately eligible for rich treatment in search results.

05 · Identity

Entity linking via sameAs

We declare, in structured data, that the organization in this release is the same organization as the one at your website, your Google Business Profile, your LinkedIn page and your other verified addresses.

Why: this is the highest-value item on the list. It is how a system decides that scattered mentions describe one business. It is not a hyperlink — it carries no link-scheme risk, and almost nobody in this category does it.

06 · Identity

Explicit subject declaration

The about property names which organization, product or place the document concerns, rather than leaving it to be inferred from the prose.

Why: inference fails on ambiguous names. Declaration doesn’t.

07 · Voice

Speakable passage marking

We mark which passages are constructed to be read aloud — the headline, the standfirst and the glance summary.

Why: voice assistants need to know which sentences survive being spoken. Most documents contain none that do.

08 · Discovery

Listed in our machine-readable site summary

Your release is added to this site’s llms.txt with a written description, alongside the issuing organization and date.

Why: it is the file AI crawlers look for to understand what a site contains. Being described there is different from merely being crawlable.

09 · Agentic

Machine-actionable contact and actions

We declare a ContactPoint and the actions available on the release, so an AI agent researching your category knows not just what you announced but how to reach you about it.

For: agents increasingly do the research step for a buyer. Being readable gets you cited; being actionable gets you contacted.

The link question, answered directly Outbound links carry rel="sponsored nofollow" at every tier, including Optimized and Network. That does not change and is not purchasable. Entity linking through sameAs is a different mechanism entirely — it is a structured-data assertion about identity, not a hyperlink, and it is the part that actually moves AI visibility.
What we need from you for Optimized Your verified profile addresses — website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and any industry registry or directory where your business is genuinely listed. We can only declare identity across addresses you actually control. There is a field for this on the submission form.

Common questions about price

Why is this cheaper than a wire service? Because we are not pretending to distribute your announcement to four hundred outlets. Wire pricing reflects a distribution model that stopped producing value over a decade ago. We publish one canonical document properly and, at the Network tier, place it where it is actually relevant.
Is there a subscription? No. Every tier is a one-time charge per release. If you publish regularly and want a standing arrangement, contact us and we will work something out — but the default is pay-as-you-go.
What if you decline my release? You are refunded in full. We review before publishing, and we would rather return your money than publish something we have come to doubt.
What am I not buying? Following links, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed pickup by outlets we do not control, or removal of the announcement label. None of those are for sale here at any price.
