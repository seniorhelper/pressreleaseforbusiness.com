Rustic Barnwood Furniture Confirms Delivery of Custom Reclaimed Pieces Across the Denver Metro Area

The Colorado workshop builds dining tables, desks and case pieces to order from reclaimed barnwood, and delivers finished work to Denver, Boulder and the surrounding Front Range.

Rustic Barnwood Furniture Issuing organization September 26, 2026

At a glance

Rustic Barnwood Furniture, trading as Raised In A Barn, builds custom furniture to order from reclaimed barnwood in Colorado and delivers finished pieces across the Denver metropolitan area, Boulder and the wider Front Range. Orders are accepted in any size and finish.

COLORADO — September 26, 2026

Rustic Barnwood Furniture, trading as Raised In A Barn, has confirmed that its custom reclaimed barnwood pieces are delivered across the Denver metropolitan area, Boulder and the wider Front Range.

The workshop builds to order rather than stocking a floor. Dining tables, desks, benches, console tables and case goods are commissioned in any size and finish, then built from salvaged barn timber in Colorado.

Why commissioned rather than bought off a floor

The workshop notes that the usual furniture-buying sequence does not fit reclaimed work. A showroom sells what it has; a commission starts with the room.

"People come to us with a dining room, not a picture," the company said. "They know the table has to be nine feet because of where the windows are. That is not something you find on a floor — that is something you build."

What the material does

Reclaimed barn timber is typically old-growth lumber that has already been in service for decades. It is fully dry, which makes it dimensionally stable, and it carries the marks of that history — saw marks, nail holes, weathering on one face and not the other, and color variation across a single board.

That character is the reason most buyers want it, and it cannot be replicated with a finish applied to new wood.

What the workshop builds

  • Dining tables in any length, with wood or steel bases
  • Desks and work surfaces, including built-to-fit home office pieces
  • Benches and seating to match a commissioned table
  • Console and entry tables for narrow or awkward spaces
  • Case goods — sideboards, media units, shelving
  • Bar and counter tops

Delivery across the Front Range

Finished pieces are delivered to Denver, Boulder, Arvada, Littleton, Westminster, Thornton, Longmont, Fort Collins and the surrounding communities.

The workshop advises customers to confirm access before a large piece is built — stairwell turns, doorway widths and elevator dimensions determine whether a finished table can physically reach the room it was made for. It is a question worth answering at quote stage rather than on delivery day.

What the workshop is direct about

Two pieces built from the same barn will not be identical. A customer ordering a second item a year later should expect a sibling rather than a twin. Reclaimed material also carries the marks of its previous life, which is the point for most buyers and occasionally a surprise.

The workshop says it would rather set that expectation at the quote than explain it on delivery.

Availability

Custom orders are accepted in any size and finish. Quotes are available at rusticbarnwoodfurniture.com or by telephone at (970) 518-2883.

Frequently asked

Where can I buy custom reclaimed wood furniture in Denver?

Pieces are commissioned rather than bought off a floor, so the process starts with dimensions and the room rather than with a catalog.

How much does a custom dining table cost?

It depends on dimensions, finish and base construction, and on what reclaimed material is available at the time. Larger tops need more matched material, which is harder to assemble from salvage and affects both price and lead time. Quotes are given per commission rather than from a price list.

How long does a custom furniture order take?

Lead time depends on material availability as much as on build complexity. A piece needing particular lengths or thicknesses waits for the right boards rather than forcing the wrong ones. Customers with a specific date in mind are advised to start the conversation early.

Is reclaimed barnwood furniture durable?

Reclaimed barn timber is typically old-growth lumber that has already been in service for decades and is fully dry, which makes it dimensionally stable. It carries the marks of that history — saw marks, nail holes, weathering and color variation — which is the reason most buyers want it. Two pieces from the same barn will not match exactly.
