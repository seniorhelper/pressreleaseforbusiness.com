A Nu Do Salon Opens Holiday Season Booking and Gift Card Sales

The Brighton salon is advising clients that color work with a specific date attached should be scheduled well ahead of it.

A Nu Do Salon Issuing organization September 15, 2026

BRIGHTON, Colorado — September 15, 2026

A Nu Do Salon has opened holiday season booking and gift card sales for clients in Brighton and the surrounding area.

The salon is advising clients that color work tied to a specific event — a wedding, a family photograph, a holiday gathering — benefits from being scheduled with room to spare.

Why timing matters for color

Significant color changes sometimes require more than one session, particularly where previous chemical work is on the hair. A client booking three days before an event has no room for a follow-up appointment if one turns out to be needed.

The salon recommends a virtual consultation ahead of the appointment for anything involving a substantial change, so that what is achievable in the available time is established before anyone is in the chair.

Gift cards

Gift cards are available through the salon's website and can be used against any service.

Availability

Appointments, virtual consultations and gift cards are available at anudosalon.com.

About A Nu Do Salon

A Nu Do Salon is a full-service hair salon in Brighton, Colorado, offering cutting, color, styling and UV tanning. The salon carries Eufora professional products and offers virtual consultations and online booking.
