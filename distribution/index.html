Start with the honest part

“Distributed to 400+ outlets” is the oldest lie in this industry

You have seen the claim. Every wire service makes it. What it usually describes is an automated feed dump into a network of low-traffic syndication sites that no human reads, built to produce an impressive-looking report rather than an audience.

It stopped working as a search tactic over a decade ago, when search engines discounted syndicated release links and began expecting them to be marked as sponsored. It never worked as a readership tactic, because nobody was reading.

So we do not make the claim. Here is what actually happens instead.

Every release, every tier

The automatic layer

This runs on publication for every announcement we carry, at every tier, without anybody pressing anything. It is a build step, not a service we perform by hand.

01

Search engine push notification

On publication your URL is submitted through IndexNow to Bing — and, in the same request, to Yandex, Naver and Seznam — rather than waiting for a crawler.

Why it matters: ChatGPT's search layer runs on Bing. Reaching Bing quickly is one of the few levers that genuinely affects AI answer visibility. The other three engines come free in the same request and matter only if you sell into those markets.

02

Real-time feed notification

Our RSS feed declares a WebSub hub and pings it on publication, so subscribers are notified in seconds rather than whenever they next poll.

Why it matters: aggregators and readers that subscribe get your announcement in seconds, not hours.

03

Syndication feeds

RSS plus a JSON API — all releases, optimized only, featured, and one endpoint per category — that any publisher, aggregator or site can consume. Plus a free embeddable widget.

Why it matters: this is the actual substrate of syndication on the web, and it is how anything reaches anywhere.

04

Machine-readable site summary

Optimized and Network releases are described in the file AI crawlers look for to understand what a site contains.

Why it matters: being described is different from merely being crawlable.

What is actually running IndexNow key verification at the domain root, a WebSub hub declared in /feed.xml, JSON endpoints under /api/, and a build step that fires both on every publish. You can verify each of these yourself rather than taking our word for it.
What nobody can automate for Google Google deprecated its sitemap ping endpoint at the end of 2023. Its Indexing API is restricted to job postings and livestream events — using it for articles breaches their terms and risks losing access. There is no legitimate button. What moves Google is a clean sitemap, a site it already crawls regularly, and genuine links pointing at the new page. We do the first, your release inherits the second, and the third is what the Network tier is for.
Network tier only

The placement layer, capped at three

At the Network tier your release is placed on named properties where the subject is genuinely relevant. Editorial pickup on Action Global News, plus up to two further properties matched by sector and geography.

Why three and not forty Because forty is the pattern search engines act against, and three relevant placements are worth more than forty irrelevant ones. We will decline to place a release somewhere it does not belong even if you ask us to.

How matching works

  • By sector. A home services announcement goes to home services properties. A technology announcement does not.
  • By geography. A Denver business reaches Colorado properties, not Hawaii ones.
  • By format. Each placement is labeled as an announcement and canonically linked back to the original here, so there is never ambiguity about which version is authoritative.
What placement is not It is not a guarantee of readership, and it is not a link-building mechanism. Links carry sponsored attributes everywhere, including on placements. What it is: your announcement appearing in more than one place, each relevant, each pointing back here.

Expectations

What to expect, honestly

Happens every time

Submitted immediately

Your URL is pushed to participating search engines the moment it publishes. The submission is deterministic infrastructure and it always happens. What each engine then does with it is their decision, not ours, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Happens over time

Subscribers accumulate

Feeds gather subscribers as a wire matures. Anyone claiming a new wire has a large subscriber base on day one is describing something else.

Does not happen

Pickup we do not control

We cannot promise a newspaper runs your story. Nobody can. Any service claiming guaranteed national pickup is selling a number, not an outcome.
