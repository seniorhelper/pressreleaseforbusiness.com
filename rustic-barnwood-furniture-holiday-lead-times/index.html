Rustic Barnwood Furniture Publishes Lead Times for Holiday Custom Orders

Commissioned pieces built from reclaimed material depend on what is in the shop, which makes early ordering more consequential than it is for catalog furniture.

Rustic Barnwood Furniture Issuing organization September 11, 2026

COLORADO — September 11, 2026

Rustic Barnwood Furniture, trading as Raised In A Barn, has published lead time guidance for customers considering commissioned pieces as holiday gifts.

The workshop notes that reclaimed material changes the scheduling question. Furniture built from newly milled lumber starts with a design and orders the wood to suit. Reclaimed work runs the other way — the boards on hand determine what can be built and at what dimensions.

What affects a lead time

  • Material availability. A piece needing particular lengths or thicknesses waits for the right boards rather than forcing the wrong ones.
  • Dimensions. Larger tops need more matched material, which is harder to assemble from salvage.
  • Finish. Fully sealed surfaces require more cure time than near-raw finishes.
  • Base construction. Steel bases involve fabrication scheduling alongside the woodwork.

What the workshop recommends

Customers with a specific date in mind are advised to start the conversation early, and to arrive with dimensions and the room rather than a picture of a finished piece. The workshop can usually say quickly whether something is achievable in the time available.

Availability

Custom orders are accepted in any size and finish. Quotes are available at rusticbarnwoodfurniture.com or (970) 518-2883.
