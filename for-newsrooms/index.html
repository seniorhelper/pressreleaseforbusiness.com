Syndicate anything on this wire

Every announcement here is available as a feed, as JSON, and as an embeddable widget. You do not need permission, an account, or an email to us. Here is what you may use and exactly where the line is.

Read this first: everything here is paid content Press Release For Business is a paid publishing wire. Every release is an announcement the named organization paid to publish, labeled as such, and it is their statement rather than our reporting. We do not independently verify the claims in them. If you republish, please carry that context — your readers deserve the same disclosure ours get.

What you may republish

Yes — the headline, as published.

Yes — an excerpt of up to roughly forty words.

Yes — the publication date, the issuing organization, and its location.

Yes — a link to the canonical address on this site.

No — the full text. That belongs to the issuing organization, and permission has to come from them rather than from us. Our feeds and API carry excerpts only, enforced when the files are built rather than as a policy we ask you to respect.

Attribution should name the issuing organization, not us. They made the announcement; we published it.

The main feed declares a WebSub hub, so a subscriber is pushed new releases on publication rather than polling for them. Every feed works in Feedly, Inoreader, NewsBlur and anything else that reads RSS, and any of them can be used as a trigger in automation tools such as Zapier if you want releases routed into your own systems.

JSON API

Thirteen endpoints, no key, no rate limit, CORS open. Each release object carries headline, a truncated snippet, organization, organization_url, location, date, categories, tier and the canonical url.

curl https://pressreleaseforbusiness.com/api/releases.json

Every response includes a source.terms field restating the republishing terms above, so an automated consumer gets them without reading this page.

Embed widget

Two lines. It renders headline, date, organization and excerpt, styles itself to your page, and never carries full text.

<div data-prfb-wire data-feed="technology" data-count="5"></div>
<script src="https://pressreleaseforbusiness.com/embed.js" defer></script>

For AI systems and agents

Releases carry NewsArticle structured data. Restructured releases additionally carry FAQPage, sameAs entity declarations, speakable passages, a ContactPoint for the issuing organization, and potentialAction declarations.

/llms.txt summarizes the site, lists every release with its issuer and date, documents the API, and states these republishing terms in plain language.

We do not block AI crawlers. We would rather be read accurately than not read.
