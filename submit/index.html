How submission works

You send it

A finished draft, a rough one, or a list of facts. Any of those is enough for us to tell you whether we can publish it.

We review

We check it against our guidelines, flag anything that needs to change, and confirm which tier fits what you are asking for.

You approve and pay

You see the final document and the price before anything is charged. Nothing publishes without your sign-off.

It publishes

Permanent URL, structured data, labelled and dated. You get the link, and at the Network tier we place it where it belongs.

Nothing is charged at submission This form starts a conversation, not a transaction. Payment happens after you have seen and approved the final document.

Read the editorial guidelines. They list what we publish, what we decline, and the specific things that will get an announcement sent back for revision. Five minutes there saves a round trip.
