What happens next

  1. We read it properly — usually within one business day.
  2. We tell you straight. If something needs changing before it can publish, you will hear exactly what and why. If we cannot publish it at all, you will hear that too, and you will not be charged.
  3. You approve the final document. Nothing goes live until you have read the version we intend to publish.
  4. Then payment, then publication. It goes live at a permanent URL and is pushed to participating search engines within minutes.

Sent something by mistake, or need to add to it? Just reply to the confirmation email — it threads straight back to us.
